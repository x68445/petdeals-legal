1 Overview

PetDeals ("we", "our", "the Service") is an automated affiliate marketing bot that creates and publishes short-form video content about pet product deals.

This Privacy Policy explains our data practices. The short version: PetDeals does not collect, store, or process personal information from viewers or users.

✅ PetDeals is a content-publishing service. We publish videos -- we do not operate a website, app, or service that requires you to create an account, submit personal information, or interact with us directly.

2 Data We Do Not Collect

PetDeals does not collect any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • IP addresses or device identifiers
  • Location data
  • Browsing history or clickstream data
  • Payment or financial information
  • Social media profile information
  • Any personally identifiable information (PII)

There is no PetDeals website, app, or login system through which we could collect user data.

3 Data We Process (Operational)

To operate the automated content pipeline, PetDeals processes the following non-personal, public data:

  • Product metadata -- product titles, prices, images, and video URLs sourced from AliExpress's public Affiliate API
  • Video metadata -- YouTube video IDs, titles, and descriptions generated and uploaded by the bot
  • Affiliate tracking parameters -- URL parameters (e.g., aff_fcid) embedded in product links for commission attribution. These parameters do not identify individual users.

None of the above data includes or is linked to any personal information about viewers or customers.

4 Third-Party APIs Used

PetDeals uses the following external APIs to operate its automated content pipeline:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy

5 Cookies & Tracking

PetDeals does not operate a website or web app and therefore does not use cookies or web tracking technologies.

✅ No cookies. No tracking pixels. No analytics scripts. No fingerprinting.

Note: When you visit YouTube, TikTok, or AliExpress through PetDeals affiliate links, those platforms may set their own cookies in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Affiliate tracking parameters in product links (e.g., aff_fcid, sk) are used by AliExpress to attribute commission to PetDeals when a purchase is made. These parameters do not uniquely identify you as an individual.

6 Data Retention

PetDeals retains only the following data, solely for the purpose of operating the automated content pipeline:

  • Product metadata (title, price, product ID, affiliate link) -- retained in a local JSON file to avoid duplicate uploads. This is non-personal, publicly available product information.
  • YouTube video IDs and upload timestamps -- retained locally to track published content. This is public metadata.
  • API credentials (OAuth tokens for YouTube and TikTok) -- stored securely on the server that operates the bot, never shared with third parties.

None of the above contains personal data about viewers, subscribers, or customers.

7 Children's Privacy

PetDeals content is general-audience pet product marketing content. We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13 (or applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), because we do not collect data from any users at all.

All PetDeals video content is marked as "Not made for kids" in compliance with YouTube's COPPA settings.

8 Platform Privacy (YouTube & TikTok)

PetDeals content is published on platforms that have their own data collection practices independent of PetDeals:

  • YouTube collects data about video views, watch time, comments, and user interactions in accordance with Google's privacy policy. PetDeals does not receive individual-level viewer data from YouTube.
  • TikTok collects data about video views, engagement, and user interactions in accordance with TikTok's privacy policy. PetDeals does not receive individual-level viewer data from TikTok.

Platform-level analytics (aggregate view counts, average watch duration) may be accessible to PetDeals as the channel operator, but this data is aggregated and does not identify individual users.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the bottom of this page reflects when changes were last made.

Given that PetDeals does not collect personal data, any changes to this policy will be administrative in nature (e.g., adding new APIs used in the pipeline, updating third-party links).

10 Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us through the comments section on any PetDeals video or via our social media profiles listed in the channel description.
